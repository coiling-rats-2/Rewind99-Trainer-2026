Rewind 99 is an action, casual, indie, and simulation game developed by Gunmetal Games and Real Fake Games. It launched in Early Access on March 11, 2026, and reached full release on August 27, 2026. Players run the world's last video rental store, expand a business, explore Reshoot Springs, complete side hustles, produce films, and survive against a streaming competitor.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Rewind 99**. This trainer provides optional runtime assistance for Rewind 99's store management, exploration, inventory, economy, and melee combat systems. It is designed for single-player use and helps reduce repetitive grinding while preserving the game's core progression and activities.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to experiment with store layouts, upgrades, inventory management, film production, exploration, side hustles, or melee encounters without extensive resource grinding.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Money** — Keeps the player's available funds from decreasing during purchases, upgrades, and other business expenses.
- **Money Multiplier** — Applies a configurable multiplier to income earned through store operations and activities.
- **Freeze Time** — Pauses the in-game clock to provide more time for store tasks and city exploration.

### 📌 Management

- **Freeze Store Funds** — Prevents the store's available funds from changing while managing stock, employees, and upgrades.
- **Instant Store Expansion** — Completes eligible store expansion and upgrade transactions without the usual financial limitation.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Stock Items** — Prevents selected movies, snacks, toys, and collectibles from being consumed when stocking or managing the store.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Player Health** — Keeps the player character's health full during non-realistic melee encounters.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Unlimited Scooter Boost** — Provides continuous access to scooter movement assistance while traveling through Reshoot Springs.
- **Movement Speed** — Allows the player to adjust movement speed while exploring the city on foot.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the trainer and then start Rewind 99. Once the player character is controllable, use the movement options to adjust walking speed or maintain scooter assistance while traveling around Reshoot Springs. Disable movement modifications before entering menus, switching areas, or joining multiplayer sessions.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate the inventory option after the store and its stock-management interface are available. Select the desired movies, snacks, toys, or collectibles and stock the store normally. Unlimited stock prevents selected quantities from decreasing during supported transactions, but it does not replace the game's purchasing or shelving interfaces.

### World & Environment

Use Freeze Time when arranging shelves, managing employees, exploring the open city, tracking NPC schedules, or pursuing side hustles. Use money-related options for store upgrades, memberships, automation, and other legitimate business expenses. Save the game normally after completing a session and turn off the trainer before loading a different save.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate Player Health before entering a melee encounter. The option is intended for the game's non-realistic melee combat and does not alter objectives or enemy behavior. Turn it off after combat to return to the standard damage system.
